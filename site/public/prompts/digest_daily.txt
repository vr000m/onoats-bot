### Structure

Structure the digest with these exact level-2 Markdown headings (`## `, each on its own line — not bold text), in this order. This mirrors the weekly synthesis so the digest renders with the same section hierarchy and in-page navigation:

- `## Day at a glance` — under the heading, 2-3 sentences summarising the day's character. State whether the day was coherent (one deep topic dominated) or scattered (many disparate topics). Mention approximate total conversation time.

- `## Decisions and commitments` — any decisions made or commitments given, one line each. Omit this section entirely if none.
- `## Open action items` — bulleted, grouped by urgency (urgent / soon / later). Omit if none.
- `## Connections` — cross-conversation links (e.g. "the SIP issue from the morning standup resurfaced in the afternoon incident review"). Omit if none.
